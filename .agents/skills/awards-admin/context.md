# International Electoral Awards - Context

## The programme

The **International Electoral Awards** are organised annually by ICPS together with a rotating co-host. The awards recognise outstanding contributions to electoral management, integrity, accessibility, and democratic governance worldwide. They are paired with a multi-day **Symposium** in the co-host country.

Judged by an independent committee of international electoral experts. Nominations are open to electoral management bodies (EMBs), officials, civil-society organisations, NGOs, charities, and individuals depending on category.

## Award categories (11 + special)

### Nomination categories (8)
1. **International Institutional Engagement Award** - election assistance/observation that increases legitimacy and public confidence. Open to international institutions, NGOs, CSOs, charities.
2. **Electoral Conflict Management Award** - managing pre/post-electoral conflicts and elections in conflict environments. Open to EMBs, officials, CSOs, NGOs.
3. **Accessibility for All Award** - equal access and participation for marginalised groups (women, persons with disabilities, minorities). Open to EMBs, officials, CSOs, NGOs.
4. **Election Management Award** - approaches overcoming country-specific challenges to guarantee secure, transparent, smooth elections. Covers registration, authentication, logistics, counting, results.
5. **Citizens' Engagement Award** - putting citizens at the heart of the process. Tackling low turnout, cynicism, distrust, legitimacy issues.
6. **First Time Voter Award** - measures optimising the first-time voter experience. Covers young voters, new citizens, inaugural elections.
7. **Electoral Ergonomy Award** - tailoring procedures to voter psychology and electorate characteristics. Ballot design, voting machines, polling-station organisation, alternative voting mechanisms.
8. **Lifetime Achievement Award** - outstanding work, deep commitment, or great sacrifice in elections and democracy.

### Non-nomination categories (2)
9. **Electoral Commissioner of the Year** - selected by the Award Committee based on in-country and international engagement.
10. **Electoral Commission of the Year** - all commissions nominated in other categories are automatically eligible. Selected by the Award Committee.

### Special awards
11. **Posthumous Meritorious Achievement Award** - special recognition for deceased individuals.
- **ICPS Excellence Award** (added 2025)
- **Citizen Statesman Award** (added 2024)

## Judging criteria (all categories)
- Originality of the initiative
- Quality of design and implementation
- Effectiveness of results
- Contribution to democratic integrity, innovation, and inclusion

## Event structure (5-day format)

### Day 0 (Saturday) - Arrivals
Arrival and registration of delegates.

### Day 1 (Sunday) - Cultural Tour & Cultural Night
- Morning: cultural tour
- Evening: cultural night

### Day 2 (Monday) - Symposium (Part 1)
- Opening and welcoming session (co-host + ICPS)
- Keynote presentations and panel discussions
- Fringe demonstrations and networking

### Day 3 (Tuesday) - Symposium (Part 2) & Awards Ceremony
- Continued panels and keynotes

### Day 4 (Wednesday) - Departures

## Post-event close-out checklist

### A. Thank-you & follow-up
- **Speaker emails:** thanks, slide-publish consent, certificate, survey
- **Winner/nominee emails:** congratulations, quote request (40-60 words), listing preferences (name/organisation/country as they wish to appear)
- **Delegate emails:** thanks, survey, photo-gallery link, resources

### B. Publication & media
- **Winners page:** 75-120-word citations, photos/logos, rationale
- **Photo gallery:** captions and alt text
- **Press release:** ICPS + co-host quotes; social posts
- **Media kit:** event summary, categories, winners, speakers, contacts

### C. Certificates & letters
- Participation and Speaker certificates (mail-merge)
- Letters of appreciation for partners and sponsors
- Awardee badges (PNG/SVG)

### D. Feedback & reporting
- Post-event surveys (delegates + speakers)
- After-Action Review (4-6 pages)
- Case studies (1,200-1,500 words per highlighted initiative)
- KPI one-pager: registration, attendance, satisfaction, web, social

### E. Logistics wrap-up
- Clean attendee data
- Confirm transfer logs closed
- Note finance items for handover
