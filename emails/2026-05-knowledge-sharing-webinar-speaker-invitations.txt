Common description:
> The session asks what it would mean for the electoral sector to share knowledge more effectively, and what a practical improvement actually looks like. Our focus is on how electoral bodies build deep institutional knowledge through every cycle, yet much of that knowledge stays locked within institutions, or disappears when staff move on. Across the sector, the same challenges are navigated in parallel, and the information that matters (legislative developments, peer experience, sector research, regulatory updates) is scattered across informal networks. The programme features an EMB perspective on the current picture, an international view from a leading academic or NGO voice, and a presentation from a vendor in the electoral industry.
